A Clearer Way Back

Campus Lost & Found combines report details, location, dates, colors, and image similarity to surface potential matches. A match is a lead, not proof of ownership. Always verify private details before arranging returns.

How It Works

  • Report Items - Submit LOST or FOUND reports with photos and details
  • Smart Matching - Our AI analyzes item similarity using visual and textual data
  • Get Notified - Receive alerts when potential matches are found
  • Secure Messaging - Message other users within the platform
  • Verify Ownership - Exchange private verification details before returns
  • Track Progress - Monitor item status and conversation history

Safety & Privacy

Your college email and student ID verify campus membership. All communications happen within the platform for security. Private verification details (marks, initials, etc.) are never shared publicly.

💡 Tip: Always verify ownership by asking about a private detail only the owner would know (e.g., "What's written on the inside?").

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I found an item but don't know who owns it?

Report it as a FOUND item. Include a clear photo and detailed description. The system will match it with LOST reports automatically.

How do I prove I own an item?

When reporting, add a private verification detail (something only you'd know). When contacted, ask the other person this detail to confirm ownership.

Can I trust the matches?

Matches are leads based on visual similarity and description. Always verify ownership through private details and meet in public spaces on campus.

Is my location data private?

You only enter where you lost/found the item (e.g., "Library", "Block A"). We don't track real-time location or share personal contact info.

What image formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. Maximum file size is 5MB. Clear, well-lit photos help improve matches.
