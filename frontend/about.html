A clearer way back to your things

Campus Lost & Found combines report details — category, location, date, color — with image similarity to surface potential matches. A match is a lead, not proof of ownership. Always verify a private detail before arranging a return.

How it works

  • Report an item. Submit a LOST or FOUND report with at least one photo.
  • Automatic matching. New reports are compared against active reports of the opposite type in the same category and location, using a color-similarity score on the uploaded photos plus how closely the location, date, and color line up.
  • Get notified. Both sides of a potential match receive a notification.
  • Message and verify. Message the other person inside the platform and confirm a private detail before meeting.
  • Mark it returned. Once ownership is confirmed, mark the match as returned.

Safety & privacy

Your college email and student ID verify campus membership. All messaging happens inside the platform. A private verification detail you set when reporting an item (for example, a mark or initials) is never shown publicly — only you can confirm it when someone contacts you.

Tip: ask the other person to describe a detail only the real owner would know before agreeing to meet.

Frequently asked questions

I found an item but don't know who owns it. What do I do?

Report it as a FOUND item with a clear photo and description. It will be compared automatically against LOST reports.

How do I prove I own an item?

Add a private verification detail when you report it. When someone contacts you about it, ask them to name that detail before you confirm.

Can I trust a match?

A match is a lead based on visual and metadata similarity, not confirmation. Always verify a private detail and meet in a public campus location.

What image formats are supported?

JPEG, PNG, and WebP, up to 5 MB per photo. Clear, well-lit photos produce better matches.
